About EcoleRonsardGPT

EcoleRonsardGPT is an educational web-app concept designed for students studying Cambridge-style subjects from Grade 1 to Grade 12.

What's included?

  • Grade selection from 1–12
  • Core Cambridge-style subjects
  • Interactive quizzes with instant marking
  • Learning explanations
  • Local progress tracking
  • Dark/light mode
  • Study GPT interface

This first version is self-contained and requires no external libraries.
